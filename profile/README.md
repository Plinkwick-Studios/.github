<h3 align="center">Making things we think are fun.</h3>

Plinkwick Studios is a place where we take our silly ideas and turn them into something fun. It is
filled with laughs, and Plinks with more personality than necessary. The studio will release more
than one game over time, and each one gets its own style.

## Games

**[Bloop! Fishing Adventure](https://plinkwickstudios.com/bloop/)** · Android · in development

Cast, hook, fight, reveal. Keep it or sell it. Bloop has opinions about both.
